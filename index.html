02

What we can offer

Useful, local and easy to join

We would offer practical, participatory activities that connect One Health, community resilience and everyday cultural exchange.

One Health

Wildlife, animal and community health

Jesper can help residents turn local observations into simple citizen-science records: wildlife health, seabird patterns, animal illness, and everyday prevention around pets, livestock, food and harbour life.

Community Design

Reducing loneliness through better rituals

Using the COM-B model, Vivi would explore what enables or blocks social connection, then facilitate practical sessions on winter routines, shared spaces, sociocracy and agile governance for local projects.

Everyday Exchange

A table, a language swap and a reason to gather

Beyond formal workshops, we would host Asian home-cooking nights, Mandarin-English-Icelandic language exchange, and running or tennis meetups: small, repeatable reasons for people to gather.

Month 01

Citizen-science kickoff: watching wildlife health, together

We would set up a simple, shared way for residents to record wildlife and seabird observations: strandings, unusual behaviour, sick animals, and seasonal patterns. Local eyes know this coastline best; we would add the epidemiological toolkit.

Month 02

Designing healthy routines for everyday village life

Practical, hands-on sessions shaped around fishing, farming, harbour life and winter routines: hand and food hygiene, safe handling of catch and game, and small behavioural nudges that make prevention and connection easier.

Month 03

Keeping animals healthy, keeping people connected

A veterinarian's open clinic of ideas paired with a community-design conversation: parasite control, vaccination basics, spotting illness early, and how shared animal care can become one more reason for neighbours to look after one another.

Format: compact, local and conversational, around 20-40 participants per session.
